ON YOUR PHONE
1. Install the Forest Network app for iOS or Android.
2. Tap "Sign in to matrix.theforestnetwork.earth".
3. Follow the steps to create your account, and enter your registration

4. Along the way, you'll be asked for a verification code from a separate

   email. That code is used to verify your email address and is different

   create your account.

IN YOUR WEB BROWSER (ELEMENT X)
